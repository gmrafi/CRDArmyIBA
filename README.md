## 🏛️ About CRD
The **Centre for Research and Development (CRD)** is the official research and academic publication wing of the **Army Institute of Business Administration (AIBA), Sylhet**, Bangladesh, located at Jalalabad Cantonment, Sylhet – 3104.

CRD oversees institutional research projects, coordinates academic seminars and methodology workshops, and publishes the official multidisciplinary peer-reviewed journal **Jalalabad Papers** (DOI Prefix: 10.68017).
