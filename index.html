The Centre for Research and Development (CRD) is the statutory academic research and publication division of Army Institute of Business Administration (AIBA), Sylhet. CRD coordinates faculty-led empirical studies, organizes advanced research workshops, and publishes the official peer-reviewed academic journal Jalalabad Papers (DOI Prefix: 10.68017).

Mission

To cultivate a disciplined research culture among faculty and students at Army IBA Sylhet through empirical investigation, research methodology workshops, and the publication of high-quality peer-reviewed scholarly research addressing socio-economic and business challenges.

Vision

To establish the Centre for Research and Development (CRD) as an active academic center for business, economic, and applied multidisciplinary research that contributes meaningfully to national policy, sustainable growth, and scholarly literature.

Institutional Profile

About Centre for Research and Development

Centre for Research and Development (CRD) serves as the institutional research division of Army Institute of Business Administration (AIBA), Sylhet, located at Jalalabad Cantonment. CRD provides structural support for academic publishing, methodological training, and research collaborations.

Journal Management

Managing the editorial workflow, double-blind peer review, and Crossref DOI registration of Jalalabad Papers.

Faculty & Student Research

Promoting collaborative research initiatives and providing research assistantship opportunities for undergraduate scholars.

Methodology Workshops

Organizing practical training on statistical software and econometric analysis tools including STATA, SPSS, and SmartPLS.

Academic Collaboration

Fostering academic linkages with national universities, development institutes, and industry practitioners.
