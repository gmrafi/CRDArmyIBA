Scholarly Vision

Institutional Framework

Statutory Mandate & Core Pillars

The Centre for Research and Development is guided by four foundational pillars designed to elevate academic rigor and empirical impact across all business disciplines.

Institutional Publishing

Overseeing the editorial governance and distribution of AIBA Sylhet's flagship *Jalalabad Papers* and affiliated internationally indexed journals.

Faculty-Student Synergy

Cultivating active research apprenticeships where undergraduate and graduate scholars co-author peer-reviewed papers under seasoned faculty mentorship.

Policy & Empirical Analytics

Delivering data-driven insights into regional FinTech adoption, green finance, and SME resilience to support industry and governmental policy formulation.

Methodological Rigor

Conducting workshops in econometric modeling, structural equation modeling (PLS-SEM), Python data science, and academic integrity ethics.
